Band of the Day
SlaveHead
Metal - medium paced, dark and heavy, but a clean sound polished and well recorded, although there are some compression defects on these mp3s. SlaveHead calls their style "Blues Metal" - but it could be described as just fairly melodic Gothic.   added 1/22/1999

Band of the Week
Mulched Losenge
Punk / Metal -- this 4-piece rock band from Denton, Texas describe themselves as "a cross between Marilyn Manson and the Butthole Surfers, with lyrics falling somewhere between Slayer and Snoop Doggy Dogg, or, sadly, 2 Live Crew". Fairly straightforward hardcore punk, but with considerable range of speed and texture, an interesting variety of vocal effects, a violin, and the occasional odd sound thrown in for good measure. A welcome expansion of the genre.   added 1/5/1999
